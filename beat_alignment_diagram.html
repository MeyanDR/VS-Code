Formula for correct alignment:
• Beat position = baseX + beatIndex × (fixedBeatWidth + beatGap)
• Step width within beat = fixedBeatWidth ÷ subdivision
• Step position = beatStartX + (stepInBeat × stepWidth)

This ensures beats always start at the same X position regardless of subdivision!
